Download chapter ZIPs

Then open the course’s download page while signed in and click the bookmark. Enter the starting chapter (normally 1). The bookmarklet finds the numbered 1080p chapter ZIPs, sorts them, and fetches one at a time under their original names. The popup shows a progress bar, bytes received, recent download speed, and estimated time remaining for the current ZIP. If its size is unknown, it shows bytes and speed without inventing a percentage or completion estimate.

Keep the page open. Use Stop, or click the bookmark again, to stop. Run it again to start from a later chapter. Already handed-off files remain in Firefox’s Downloads.

Each ZIP must finish fetching before it appears in Firefox’s Downloads; large archives are temporarily buffered by the browser. Allow multiple downloads if prompted. This limits this queue to one network transfer at a time, but does not cap bandwidth or track whether you cancel a Save dialog.
